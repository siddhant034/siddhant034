I build software, explore ideas, and enjoy understanding how things work under the hood.

Frontend engineering is my strongest foundation, but my interests extend beyond the browser. I enjoy working across application interfaces, APIs, integrations, developer workflows, and the engineering decisions that make software useful and maintainable.

My GitHub reflects an ongoing journey of learning and building: experiments, practical applications, technical explorations, and projects from different stages of my development as an engineer.

---

## How I approach engineering

- **Build to understand.** I learn by turning ideas into working software.
- **Stay curious.** I'm interested in the systems and decisions behind the interface, not just the interface itself.
- **Value fundamentals.** Tools evolve, but strong underlying concepts continue to matter.
- **Keep experimenting.** Not every project needs to become a product; experiments are valuable in their own right.
- **Improve through iteration.** Good engineering comes from testing ideas, reflecting, and learning from what doesn't work.
